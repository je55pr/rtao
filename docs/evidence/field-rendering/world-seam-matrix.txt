Concrete next traversal
-----------------------
The seam graph gives the following boundary-connected field chain from Peach Town to Fuji City:

  223 --N/S--> 221 --W/E--> 220 --N/S--> 113
  Peach Town              Bridge              Fuji City

The blue road triangles in each of FLD/223, FLD/221, FLD/220 and FLD/113 union into one connected polygon per field, so these are not unrelated boundary stubs that merely happen to touch the required edges. The authored minimap road surface is connected through each intermediate field.

The ordinary render mesh also agrees at all three route seams. Matching edge coordinates/heights found directly in the raw MSCALF 8 geometry:

  223 N <-> 221 S : 3 unique matching axis/height samples; road surface Y = 30
  221 W <-> 220 E : 5 unique matching axis/height samples; road surface Y = 5
  220 N <-> 113 S : 18 unique matching axis/height samples; principal road surface Y = 25 (additional coincident authored layers also exist)

This is still not a claim that the player has successfully driven the complete route in the MonoGame runtime. Collision/vehicle traversal through all three seams remains the next live validation target.

Conclusion
----------
The alternating +800 source-row stagger and current filename-to-grid mapping are supported by multiple independent authored road boundaries. A visually surprising camera-relative all-world render is therefore not sufficient evidence for replacing the canonical topology transform.
